# NyayaSetu AI

NyayaSetu AI is a user-friendly platform that makes the Indian legal system more accessible for ordinary citizens. It combines AI-powered tools with practical utilities to help users understand their rights, prepare documents, and take informed legal actions.

The platform features an AI legal chatbot, case-law search and summarization, complaint letter generators, cost estimators, evidence checklists, and a legal glossary. These tools provide guidance in simple language while supporting multi-language access to reach users across different regions of India. NyayaSetu AI is designed to assist users and does not replace professional legal advice.

To use the project, clone the repository and install the required dependencies for the frontend and backend. Start the servers to access the platform locally. NyayaSetu AI provides structured legal knowledge, interactive guides, and templates to help users navigate the legal system efficiently.
